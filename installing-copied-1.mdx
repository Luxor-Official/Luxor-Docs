A guide on installing MOON into your game!

<Steps>
  <Step title="Aquire MOON">
    Head to our roblox hub and buy MOON. Then you will get a direct message from **Vault** with the download file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the file">
    Download the file from **Vault** and open roblox studio then drop the `.rbxm` file into workspace and right click it and make it a `LocalPlugin`. To install Moon into your game click **Moon Inject**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configuration">
    To configure MOON's settings you must click the **Moon Config** button under plugins.

    1. Once clicked a widget will open on to your screen.
    2. You will see the first option is Webhook URL and you need to paste the webhook url there.
    3. Next are misc settings which are pretty self explanatory
    4. Once configured to your liking click the **Save** button at the bottom of the widget. Once saved you will see a module script appear in **ServerStorage** which holds you config data.
    5. To change you settings re-open the widget again and change it to your liking and click **Save** again and it will auto update the module.

    If you don't know how to get a webhook url follow this video → [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fKksxz2Gdnc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fKksxz2Gdnc)

    <Danger>
      NEVER SHARE YOUR WEBHOOK URL TO SOMEONE YOU DON'T TRUST.
    </Danger>
  </Step>
</Steps>
